# MAS Risk Governance Framework 🛡️

**Open-Source Risk Management & Procurement Standard for Multi-Agent AI Systems (EU AI Act Compliant)**

## The Paradigm Shift
Traditional AI compliance focuses on static models and Prompt Injection. In 2026, the deployment of **Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)** shifts the threat landscape. Autonomous agents introduce emergent misalignment, covert channels, and architectural vulnerabilities that standard IT procurement contracts (SLA/OPZ) fail to cover.

This framework provides C-level executives, Risk Officers, and Procurement Leaders with actionable tools to secure MAS deployments under **Article 15 of the EU AI Act**.
