# MAS Risk Governance Framework

**Translating Multi-Agent System (MAS) risks into operational controls for procurement and compliance.**

## The Objective
AI Governance is entering the agentic era. When AI systems can access tools, delegate tasks, and consume computational resources autonomously, traditional model-centric compliance is no longer sufficient. 

This repository serves as a practical knowledge base for organizations operating in high-risk or regulated environments. Its core focus is bridging the gap between abstract regulatory requirements (such as the EU AI Act) and technical realities by providing a functional translation layer:

**Regulation → Risk → Architecture → Procurement → Contract → Operational Control**

## Governance Artefacts
This framework is an evolving open-source project. The goal is to provide actionable controls and risk boundaries rather than theoretical commentary.

* 📖 [**MAS Threat Glossary**](./1_MAS_Threat_Glossary.md)
  *A conceptual breakdown of 10 system-level vulnerabilities (e.g., Emergent Misalignment, Agentic Collusion) that traditional AI audits and red-teaming often miss.*
  
* ⚖️ [**Procurement Clauses (OPZ/SWZ)**](./2_Procurement_Clauses_EU.md)
  *Draft contractual controls (Zero-Trust Sandboxing, Token FinOps, Semantic Monitoring) designed to constrain vendor AI systems and ensure compliance with EU AI Act Article 15 (Robustness & Cybersecurity).*

## The Approach
Less abstract compliance. More operational control. 
These artifacts are published to make governance reasoning, technical controls, and assumptions visible—and to continuously improve them through scrutiny.
